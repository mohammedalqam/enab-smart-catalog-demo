# Asset provenance

All product imagery is original generated concept photography created for this demo with the built-in image generation tool. There are no sourced ENAB CANDY photographs, official logos, real branded wrappers, real prices or product claims. No external hotlinked assets are used.

- `public/products/p01.webp` through `p30.webp`: individual cells extracted from an original 6-column, 5-row photographic atlas, optimized to WebP. Cell order exactly follows the product seed order: six chocolates; six candies; five snacks; four drinks; four biscuits; three boxes; two bundles.
- Atlas prompt: “A precisely aligned 6×5 photographic contact sheet, each square on warm ivory, centered distinct product still lifes, no labels or logos. Milk chocolate, dark chocolate, cocoa truffles, hazelnut pralines, white chocolate, almond clusters; gummy bears, sour rainbow belts, strawberry hearts, peach rings, marshmallows, spiral lollipops; potato chips, nachos, popcorn, pretzels, mixed nuts; strawberry lemonade, mango drink, grape drink, lemon mint drink; cookies, vanilla wafers, cocoa wafers, butter biscuits; candy box, chocolate box, mixed sweet box; movie snack bundle, sour candy bundle.”
- `public/hero.webp` and `public/share.jpg`: original studio still life of a cream paper cone with gummy bears, strawberry hearts, peach rings, sour rainbow belts and a pink spiral lollipop on pale blush pink. Prompt requested photorealistic textures, natural soft light and no text, branding or people.
- `public/icon.svg` and the grape brand mark: original minimal vector design created in source code for the concept. It is not the store's official logo.
- Cairo: self-hosted font via `@fontsource/cairo`, under the SIL Open Font License. The package includes the font license. Lucide: ISC-licensed icon library.

These images are explanatory concept assets and must be replaced with authorized real product photography before presenting an official store catalog.
